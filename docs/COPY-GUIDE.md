# OffScroll copy guide

How the app talks, and why. Use it whenever you write or change text the user sees. The ideas come from well-studied
findings in psychology and usability research; they are good habits, not guarantees, so when something matters,
test it on real people.

## The voice
Calm, warm, direct. A friend who respects your time. OffScroll's whole promise is *less pull, more control*, so the
words never pressure, hurry or guilt.

## Principles
1. **Easy to read feels trustworthy.** Short, common words and one idea per line are processed faster and are judged
   more honest. Use "Floating window", not "System Pop-up Player (PiP)". No jargon (codec, scoped storage, bitrate).
2. **Say the benefit, then the step.** "Show my videos" beats "Grant permission". Name the outcome the person wants.
3. **Explain why before you ask.** A permission request that says what is read, what is not, and that it can be
   changed is accepted more often and trusted more. Always say what is NOT done (not uploaded, only videos).
4. **Keep the person in charge.** Offer a real choice and an easy way out ("Keep it", "Skip"). Commands and
   countdowns provoke the urge to push back (reactance).
5. **Errors: calm, blameless, one next step.** Say what happened and what to do. A threatening or vague message
   raises stress and makes people give up. No "Error", "Failed", "Invalid", "Oops", no blaming "you did X wrong".
6. **Confirm the small win, right away.** Specific, immediate feedback ("Saved to Watch Later") closes the loop.
   Keep it quiet. Never use variable rewards, streaks, fake scarcity or guilt: they are the doom-scroll toolkit this
   app exists to avoid.
7. **Positive framing and plain verbs.** "Keep it" instead of "Cancel" on a delete dialog makes the safe choice
   obvious. Prefer "Nothing here yet" to "No results found".
8. **Be honest, even when it costs a little appeal.** Say "Plays offline", not "100% offline", because saving
   from the internet needs a connection. Say "Nothing was deleted" only when true.
9. **Sentence case.** It reads faster than Title Case or ALL CAPS. Reserve small caps labels for tiny captions.
10. **Emoji sparingly:** one, at the end, only to confirm a happy moment.

## Words we use
| Use | Never use |
|---|---|
| Save (media from the internet) | Download (except for the app or engine itself), Retry |
| Try again | Retry |
| Show my videos | Grant permission |
| Floating window | PiP, system pop-up player |
| Fit to screen | Aspect ratio / fit mode |
| Offline | Vault (jargon) |

## Quick checks before you ship text
- Could a tired person on a slow day understand it in two seconds?
- Does it say what happens next, or only what went wrong?
- Is every claim true on a phone with no signal, and on one with?
- Did the same idea keep the same word everywhere?
- If there is a translation (Hindi, Telugu), update it in the same change.
